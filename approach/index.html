Approach

How we build, and how we keep building.

We make software meant to leave someone's day better than it found it. These are the commitments that decide what we build, what we refuse to build, and what we are willing to claim — including how we pay for it.

Private where it counts

Wherever the work can happen on your own device, that is where we do it. EverHeard captions speech and recognises sounds entirely on your phone or Mac: no account, no server listening, nothing recorded.

Not everything can work that way, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. Some tasks need more capability than a phone has, and when you ask for one, that request leaves the device. In those cases we hold to three rules: send the least that will do the job, strip what identifies you before it goes, and keep nothing afterwards. The result comes back to your device or to storage we control — not to an advertising market, and not into training data.

We apply the protections the technology allows, and we tell you where the line falls in each product. What we will not do is promise a level of privacy the software cannot actually deliver.

Accessible by design

We design for the people most often left out, and everyone else benefits. Captions that keep the speaker's own language rather than translating it away. Alerts you can feel instead of hear. Interfaces that respect your text size, your contrast and your preference for less motion.

Accessibility is not a feature we add at the end. In our flagship product it is the entire point.

Honest about limits

Software that is confidently wrong is worse than software that admits doubt. Each of our products says this in its own way, because they fail in different ways: EverHeard must never fabricate certainty for someone who cannot check it against what they heard; ResumeCraft_AI turns gaps into questions rather than inventions; Myth MacroMKR never promises what its code doesn't do, and says when its knowledge may be out of date.

The same discipline governs how we describe our own work. Every status on this site is the real one. A product approved but not yet released says exactly that. A feature still being built is never described as available. We publish no user, download or revenue figures, because we would rather say nothing than imply something.

Resilient by construction

We build on proven, open, on-device technology, so our products keep working when the network doesn't, recover from their own failures, and don't depend on somebody else's pricing decisions to stay alive. A self-tuning microphone that adapts to a noisy room and recovers on its own is worth more than a feature list.

Built to last means available when you need it, and still working years from now.

Funded so the work can continue

We are a for-profit company, on purpose. We would rather charge honestly for what costs money than quietly sell the person using our software.

Some of what we make belongs to the community rather than to a price list. Where the good a product does outweighs what we could earn from it, we commit to keeping it free — and we fund that commitment deliberately, through grants, sponsorship, institutions, donations, and the revenue of the products that do charge. Working with funding partners is how a free promise is kept rather than merely made.

The test is the work, not the wallet. This isn't about who can afford what; it's about what shouldn't be charged for in the first place.

So we won't tell you everything we make will be free forever. We will tell you, for each product, what it costs and who pays — and where we have committed to keep something free, we name it and we honour it.

Our commitment today: EverHeard's core is free, with no paywall, no nagging and no feature held back for payment — because hearing what is happening around you isn't something anyone should have to buy. Funding is our job, not the user's.

How the work gets done

Product decisions, architecture, domain logic and validation on real devices are ours. Code implementation is AI-generated under direction, then reviewed and corrected. It lets a small studio hold a high standard across several products, and we say so plainly rather than implying a larger team.

Working with us? We build for organisations too, on commercial terms — and we partner with funders and research institutions on work meant to stay free for the people who need it. Start a conversation.
