Lanternfall: Isles of Dusk

Draw one line of light. The Drift goes dark each night. As Wren, the last apprentice keeper, you row from island to island and bring the light back.

Coming to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

How it plays

Drag one unbroken line of flame from the hearth through every lantern before the wick burns out. Your line can't cross itself. Puddles put the flame out, oil jars feed the wick, and the wind carries it a step for free. Each lantern you light spreads its glow across the island.

What's in the game

  • 30 hand-made story islands in two chapters, The Near Isles and The Lantern Coast
  • A new island every day, free
  • The Far Reaches: islands that keep coming after island 30 and get harder as you go, with a new look every ten islands and a gold-flame challenge every fifth
  • Hints that light the next few tiles when you're stuck
  • Play at your own pace: the story islands have no timers

Free to start

The first 10 islands and the daily island are free. One purchase of the full game opens all 30 story islands, adds 10 hints and removes the ads between islands. Hint packs and keys for the Far Reaches are optional, and every purchase says exactly what you get. You can also choose to watch an ad for a reward, such as a key, a hint or one more island a day.

Privacy

No account needed. Ads aren't personalised unless you allow tracking. Your keys and Far Reaches progress sync through your own iCloud. Read the full Lanternfall Privacy Policy.

Lanternfall is meant for players aged 13 and over.
