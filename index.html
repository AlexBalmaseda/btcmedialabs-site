Our portfolio

Each one takes on a different corner of everyday life — accessibility, gaming, careers — and shares the same DNA: private, on-device where it counts, and built for the person using it.

Accessibility · Flagship Approved · releasing shortly

EverHeard

Live captions and sound alerts for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing. It turns speech and the sounds around you — a doorbell, a knock, a smoke alarm, your name across the room — into captions and instant alerts. Everything is processed on your device, and captions stay in the language actually spoken.

How we work

Small team. High craft. People at the centre.

We keep the team small and the standards high, and we use proven, open, on-device technology so our products stay private, work offline after a one-time download, and remain sustainable. We say plainly what is finished and what is still ahead.

On-device first.

Intelligence runs on your hardware, not in a data centre. Private, fast, and able to work offline after a one-time download.

Accessible to everyone.

We design for the people most often left out. If it works for them, it works better for everyone.

Built to last.

No per-user cloud costs and no rented intelligence, so our software can keep serving people for years.

Research

Teaching software to know when it doesn't know.

A hearing person catches a wrong caption because they heard the words. A Deaf reader cannot. So a confidently wrong caption is worse than none at all. Our research programme is about systems that can say "unclear" instead of guessing — and we are looking for a university partner to pursue it.
