Our portfolio

A small studio, a growing family of products.

Each one tackles a different corner of everyday life — accessibility, gaming, careers — and shares the same DNA: private, on-device where it counts, and built for the person using it.

Accessibility · FlagshipLive · iPhone, iPad, Mac

EverHeard

Real-time multilingual captions and environmental sound awareness for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing. It turns speech and the sounds around you — a doorbell, a knock, a smoke alarm, your name across the room — into live captions and instant alerts. Everything runs on-device: nothing is ever recorded or sent to the cloud.

How we work

Small team. High craft. People at the center.

We keep the team small and the standards high. We use proven, open, on-device technology so our products are private, work offline, and stay sustainable — and we build in the open about what's live today versus what's still ahead.

On-device first.

Intelligence runs on your hardware, not a data center — private, fast, and offline-capable.

Accessible to everyone.

We design for the people most often left out. If it works for them, it works better for everyone.

Built to last.

No per-user cloud costs, no rented intelligence — software that can keep serving people for years.
