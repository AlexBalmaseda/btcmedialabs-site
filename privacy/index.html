Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and has no accounts or sign-in. We cannot build a profile of you from it.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the technical information your browser sends in order to deliver the page, such as your IP address. That data is handled under GitHub's own privacy practices, and we do not receive it.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and our reply so we can carry on the conversation. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we don't share it.

Our products

Each product states its own privacy terms, and they are stricter than this page. EverHeard processes speech and sound on your device; see everheard.app/privacy. Myth MacroMKR's add-on has no network access at all; see its privacy page.
