Boilerplate

BTC Media Labs LLC is a New York software studio building privacy-respecting, human-first technology. Its products run on the person's own device rather than in the cloud, and include EverHeard, an accessibility app providing live captions and sound alerts for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing; Myth MacroMKR, a free World of Warcraft add-on and companion app; and ResumeCraft AI, a career companion in development.

Company facts

  • Legal name: BTC Media Labs LLC
  • Formed: 13 August 2026, New York
  • Founder & CEO: Alex Balmaseda
  • Sister company: Business Transformation Consulting LLC, an advisory firm founded in 2014
  • Contact: alex@btcmedialabs.com

Product one-liners

  • EverHeard — live captions and sound alerts for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing, processed entirely on the device. In TestFlight beta with testers.
  • Myth MacroMKR — a free World of Warcraft add-on for macros, keybinds and talents, with an optional desktop companion. Publicly distributed on CurseForge and GitHub.
  • ResumeCraft_AI — a career companion built around one living record of your career. In development.
